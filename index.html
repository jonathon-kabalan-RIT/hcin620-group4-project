Stakeholders & Potential Users:

Potential users:

Older adults who regularly use digital platforms such as social media, messaging applications, video platforms, or other online information sources.


Stakeholders:

-> Older adults and their families
-> Digital-literacy educators and community organizations
-> Organizations that provide services and support to older adults
-> Designers and developers of digital platforms
-> Researchers studying AI literacy, misinformation, and aging

The Problem We Plan to Solve:

As AI-generated content becomes more realistic, older adults may face increasing difficulty determining whether the content they encounter online is authentic or AI-generated. This can lead to older adults being misinformed or even scammed.

The Solution:

We propose designing an accessible AI-content literacy tool for older adults that helps users evaluate whether digital content may be AI-generated or human-created. We will gamify learning for older adults in short interactive activities that help them compare and detect AI vs. human-generated content. This tool may also have the ability for older adults to upload different types of media to help assess its origin.
