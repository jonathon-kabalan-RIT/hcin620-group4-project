Overview

After conducting a card sorting activity with a variety of participants, we synthesized the results into a sitemap that organizes the product around how users naturally group and label content. Using that structure, we built a low-fidelity prototype covering three key user tasks, with all screens needed to complete each one. The prototype focuses on navigation, core tasks, and visual hierarchy rather than aesthetics.
