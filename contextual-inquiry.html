Overview

At this stage in the project, we had established our project focus, and needed to begin collecting user data to inform our decisions moving forward.


We conducted two interviews with potential users. Both interviewees were recently retired (within the last 5 years), and fell within our target demographic age range. The interviewees varied in their expressed comfort levels, usage, and familiarity with Generative AI.


Following the interviews, we regrouped for an interpretation session, and created an affinity diagram from our notes. Finally, a Contextual Inquiry report was created to document our findings, identify challenges, and consider potential areas of improvement for future research.
