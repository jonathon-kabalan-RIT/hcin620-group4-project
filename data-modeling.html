Overview

After conducting user interviews, creating an affinity diagram, and writing the contextual inquiry report, we had to translate the insights and data gathered into actionable design guidance. To do this, we created two personas, each with their own empathy map. In the next step, we used these data models to create user stories, and derive user requirements.

Persona Two: Edwina


Why empathy maps as our second data model?

We chose an empathy map as our second data model because our interviews ultimately centered on interviewees' feelings toward, and experiences with AI generated content. The goal of our proposed tool is to help people, and to do that, we need to identify where they are coming from, what they currently think and feel, and why. As a group, we decided that empathy maps were best suited to sort these details out.
